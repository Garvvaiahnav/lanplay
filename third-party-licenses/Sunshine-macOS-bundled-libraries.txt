Libraries inside Sunshine.app (macOS build)

LanPlay ships LizardByte's Sunshine.app unmodified (Sunshine v2026.914.233613, GPL-3.0; see Sunshine-GPL-3.0.txt and
SOURCE-OFFER.md). The app brings its own copies of these libraries in Contents/Frameworks. Nothing in them was
changed by LanPlay.

Qt 6.11.1 (QtCore, QtDBus, QtGui, QtSvg, QtWidgets and the plugins in Contents/PlugIns): LGPL-3.0 (some Qt modules and
tools use other licenses; see the Qt project). Source: https://download.qt.io/official_releases/qt/6.11/ and
https://code.qt.io/cgit/qt/qtbase.git. License text: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.txt. The Qt libraries are
dynamic libraries inside the app; you may replace them with a modified build of the same Qt version.

The other libraries in Contents/Frameworks (OpenSSL, ICU, GLib, HarfBuzz, FreeType, libpng, libjpeg, zstd, PCRE2,
libdbus, miniupnpc, libb2, double-conversion, md4c, graphite2, libintl) are open-source projects under their own
licenses (Apache-2.0, ICU, LGPL-2.1+, MIT, BSD, FreeType, libpng and similar). Their source is available from each
project and from the Sunshine build recipe at https://github.com/LizardByte/Sunshine/tree/v2026.914.233613.
